## The problem in one sentence

Whether a second person reading the same records changes which works a review includes.

## What the field calls it

| Level | The field's name | First met in |
|---|---|---|
| the practice | **double screening**, **independent reviewers** | Edwards 2002; Waffenschmidt 2019 |
| the agreement | **interrater reliability**, **Cohen's kappa** | McHugh 2012 |
| the report | **how many reviewers screened each record** | Page 2021 |

## What practice calls it

- A body that sets standards for its reviews writes of two people working independently (Lefebvre 2025).
