## Strand 0: existing reviews

- Across four evaluations of single screening, the median share of studies missed was 5%, up to 58% (Waffenschmidt 2019, abstract, secondary). Measured.

## Strand 1: how many does one reader miss (Q1)

- One reviewer alone missed 8% of eligible reports on average, and pairs 0 to 1% (Edwards 2002, abstract, secondary). Measured.
- The median missed was 3% for six experienced reviewers and 13% for three less experienced (Waffenschmidt 2019, abstract, secondary). Measured.

## Strand 2: what a standard requires (Q2)

- At least two people work independently on eligibility. One person may screen titles and abstracts (Lefebvre 2025, page, read). Practice.
- A review reports how many reviewers screened each record and whether they worked independently (Page 2021, item 8). Convention.
- No study of a second rating of a quality score was found (protocol, F10, F11). Not found, in these searches.
