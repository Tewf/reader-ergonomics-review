# Against

The evidence against a second reader, and the disagreements. Sources are in [review](review.md).

**Shape:** [against](../../against.md)

## Against "always two readers"

- Single screening by an experienced reviewer is called a possible shortcut for rapid reviews (Waffenschmidt 2019, abstract, secondary).
- The standard-setter itself lets one person screen titles and abstracts (Lefebvre 2025, page, read).

## Disagreements

| Point | One side | Other side |
|---|---|---|
| Is one reader enough? | pairs missed 0 to 1% (Edwards 2002) | an experienced reader missed 3% (Waffenschmidt 2019) |

- A possible reason: the readers' experience differs between the studies.

## Against the sources themselves

- Both studies screen clinical records; the review's subject is software engineering.
- Both figures were read from abstracts.
