# State of the art

Step 3 of the review. For each question, the best evidence, kept apart from the practice most adopted. Upper bounds (what was achieved) are kept apart from limits (what was shown not to work). Sources are in [review](review.md).

## Q1 The files

| Record | Holder | What it says | Grade |
|---|---|---|---|
| Most adopted | Page 2021 | a 27-item checklist for the report of a review, with a flow diagram | convention, by consensus |
| For a search | Rethlefsen 2021 | 16 items for reporting the search | convention, by Delphi consensus |
| For grey sources | Garousi 2019 | 14 guidelines, from the decision to include grey sources to the report | convention |

## Q2 The grey score

## Q3 The second rater

| Record | Holder | What it says | Grade |
|---|---|---|---|
| Best evidence | Edwards 2002 | single reviewers missed 8% of eligible reports on average; pairs missed 0 to 1% | measured |
| Upper bound on single screening | Waffenschmidt 2019 | median 3% missed by experienced reviewers | measured |
| Limit | Waffenschmidt 2019 | 13% missed by less experienced ones; findings changed in 3 of 7 | measured |
| Most adopted | Lefebvre 2025 | two people on eligibility; one may screen titles and abstracts | convention |
| A model as reader | Khraisha 2024 | on par in some tasks; adjusted for chance, screening none to moderate | measured |
