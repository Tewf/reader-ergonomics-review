# Frame: the problem and its names

Step 1 of the review, read on 2026-11-03. It states the problem, gives the field's names beside the practitioners', and says whether the repository's name matches.

## The problem in one sentence

What a literature review keeps, in which files, so that a reader and a program can check its search, its scores and its count of sources.

## What the field calls it

The field has no single name for the whole. Five literatures meet, each with its own word.

| Level | The field's name | First met in |
|---|---|---|
| the method | **systematic literature review** (SLR), **systematic review** | Kitchenham 2009; Page 2021 |
| the method with grey sources | **multivocal literature review** (MLR) | Garousi 2019 |
| the report of a review | **reporting guideline**, **checklist** (PRISMA) | Page 2021 |
| the report of the search | **search reporting** (PRISMA-S) | Rethlefsen 2021 |
| the search by citations | **snowballing**, backward and forward; **hybrid search** | Wohlin 2014; Wohlin 2022 |
| the second reader | **double screening**, **independent reviewers** | Edwards 2002; Waffenschmidt 2019 |
| the agreement of two readers | **interrater reliability**, **Cohen's kappa** | McHugh 2012 |
| the grade of a grey source | **quality assessment of grey literature** | Garousi 2019 |
| the count of sources in and out | **flow diagram** | Page 2021 |
| the plan kept before the work | **protocol**, **registration** | Page 2021 |

## What practice calls it

- A body that sets standards for its own reviews calls the minimum expectations **MECIR** standards (Lefebvre 2025).
- A **baseline** is what a review compares its subject with. The field has no word for the file; the closest is a comparator in the review's question.
- **Against** is the evidence that contradicts the claim. The field's nearest words are limitations of the evidence and reporting bias (Page 2021, items 14 and 23).
- The family's other words are the names of its files: frame, protocol, sources, review, state of the art, position and references.

## Does the repository's name match?

- "Review" is the field's own word, and the repository means the literature review.
- The name is not renamed. The maintainer decides.
- The topic folder takes the genre's name, review-genre.
