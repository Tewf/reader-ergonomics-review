# Position

Step 4 of the review. What is known, what was not found, and what this repository asks. Each "not found" is a statement about these searches, not about the world. Terms are in [frame](frame.md).

## Known

- A second reader catches what one misses when screening records: 8% on average in one study (Edwards 2002), a median of 5% across four (Waffenschmidt 2019).
- Grey sources change the result of a review: published trials showed a larger effect (Hopewell 2007).
- A search by protocol finds a minority of sources; citations find more (Greenhalgh 2005; Badampudi 2015).
- Reports of reviews are checked by checklists that persons fill in (Page 2021; Rethlefsen 2021).

## Not found in these searches

- A study of how reliably two persons score a grey source on Garousi 2019's criteria.
- A study of the second rating of a quality score, as against a screening decision.
- A released program that checks the report of a review; PRISMA-Check is in development (PRISMA Executive 2026).
- A study of which files of a review help a reader.
- A standard set of files for a review in software engineering.

## The four questions

| Question | Answer from the evidence | Grade |
|---|---|---|
| The files | The guidelines ask for contents, not files. PRISMA items 24 to 27 and 14 to 15 have no file in the nine reviews; against, position and baseline are in no guideline. | convention |
| The grey score | Garousi 2019 gives the criteria and an example threshold. The reliability of the score is unmeasured. | convention |
| The second rater | Evidence supports a second reader on screening in health. For a quality score it supports nothing yet; the agreement should be reported, not assumed. | supported with caveat for screening; convention for scoring |
| The checks | A program can decide the files, the arithmetic, the strings and counts, the figures' keys and the report of agreement. It cannot decide whether a score or a search is right. | convention |

## What this repository asks

- Does a review that a program checks for these parts differ, in what a reader can reproduce, from one that no program checks?
- How reliably do two persons score the same grey source on the 20 criteria, and does the score change what a review includes?
- Do the files against and baseline change what a reader concludes?

## What the shapes must decide

- The parts of each of the nine files, and the label a check reads.
- Which parts a program checks, and which are left to a reader.
- Where the references live: in the review, or once for the repository.
