# The literature review as a genre: what the field measures about searching, scoring, reporting and checking one

A first-pass multivocal review, read on 2026-11-03. It asks what research and practice know about the files a review keeps, the grey score, the second reader and the checks, and it ends on a named baseline.

- It holds 25 sources: 23 formal and 2 grey; no grey source scores below the threshold.
- Formal: 12 measured or descriptive, 6 convention, 2 supported with caveat, 3 located only. Eighteen were read from the abstract, so their figures are marked (secondary).
- Grey: one tier 1 (17.5 of 20) and one tier 2 (10 of 20). One reviewer scored both; there is no second rater.
