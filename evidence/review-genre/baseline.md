# Baseline

Step 5 of the review: the baseline the genre is compared against. It is the family's practice as it stands, written down for this review. Evidence is in [review](review.md).

## B1: the nine reviews as they stand

B1 is the nine reviews of the AI-augmented project and the project-method topic, at the commits the [shapes](../../shapes/README.md) link. Each keeps the files frame, protocol, sources, review, against, state of the art, position and baseline. None keeps a `references.md`: all cite one shared bibliography.

1. **One folder per question**, with the eight files and a front page.
2. **The protocol** lists the strings with their counts, the stopping rule and what was not searched.
3. **Sources** gives the flow, then one row per source, formal with a grade and grey with a score of 20 and its eight subtotals.
4. **Review** states each finding with its figure, its key and where it was read.
5. **A second rater** scored the grey sources in some reviews, and the agreement was reported.

## Compared with what the guidelines ask

| Guideline item | In B1 | Fit |
|---|---|---|
| Full search strategy (Page 2021, item 7) | strings with counts in the protocol | matches; one of nine has no table of strings |
| Reviewers and independence (item 8) | "one reviewer" in the limits; second rater in some | matches in part |
| Flow diagram (item 16a) | a flow table in sources | matches |
| Excluded studies with reasons (item 16b) | a "left out" list in some | matches in part |
| Registration and protocol (item 24) | the protocol file; no statement on registration | lacks the statement |
| Support, competing interests (items 25, 26) | none | lacks |
| Availability of data (item 27) | the repository is public; not said | lacks the statement |
| Reporting bias, certainty (items 14, 15, 21, 22) | grades per claim | partly, by the four grades |
| Quality of grey sources (Garousi 2019, Table 7) | eight subtotals and a total | matches |
| Agreement between persons (Garousi 2019, section 5.2) | reported when two raters exist | goes beyond |

## What B1 lacks

- A program that checked it. The [checks](../../checks/README.md) here are the first to read B1; their run on the nine is in the front page.
- A statement of registration, support, competing interests and availability.
- A measure of the reliability of the grey score.

## How it is measured

- The findings of the five checks on B1, before and after a review follows the shapes.
- The agreement of two raters on the same grey sources, when two exist.

The baseline is named: B1. The shapes and checks are the next job; [position](position.md) lists what they must decide.
