# Five steps, in order

**Claim:** a review runs frame, review, state of the art, position and baseline in that order, and is finished when it can name the baseline it compares against.

- **Grade:** convention, untested. No study compared a review in this order with one in another.
- **Evidence:** [position](../../evidence/review-genre/position.md); [baseline](../../evidence/review-genre/baseline.md); [Garousi 2019](../../evidence/bibliography.md), the phases of its guidelines.
- **Check:** `review-files` (exact): the files of the five steps exist; their order and the finish are judgment, answered by the maintainer.
- **Reader styles:** none.

## What it asks

- Frame first: say the problem in one sentence and find what the field calls it.
- Review next, surveys first; then the state of the art, the position and the baseline.
- Stop only when the baseline is named.

## Why

- A search cannot start before the problem has the field's name; the position cannot be written before the records.
- A review with no baseline cannot be compared with anything.

## Limits

- Garousi 2019 and PRISMA 2020 order the phases of a review (plan, conduct, report), not these five steps.
- The step of position is the family's: no guideline names it.
